## 4. Pitfalls hit this session

- `rerun-multimap` does not skip `kb_count` (SW-22). For a cell-calling-only
  change, copy the run dir, edit `config.yaml`, and call `detection.run` in
  process (`hpv16_gse189670/ed_driver.py` + `ed.sbatch`).
- Under `set -o pipefail`, `zcat | head` exits with SIGPIPE. Wrap it:
  `(zcat f || true) | head`.
- SLURM copies the batch script at submit time. To fix a queued script, cancel
  and resubmit.
- New PLAN IDs: grep the highest number first. SW-21 was already taken (the knee
  defect is SW-23).
- Background wait loops are capped at 30 min / 2 h. Re-arm them; don't trust
  `pgrep -f` in a loop (it can match itself).
- `docs/output_reference.md` is sha256-pinned in
  `analysis/v3_artifact_inventory.tsv`. Any edit needs a re-pin commit
  (`git_sha` and `artifact_sha256` set to the editing commit).
- Do not `git add -A`. `.living/INDEX.md`, `LOG_REGISTRY.md` and
  `docs/review-clear-execute-*` carry another session's uncommitted edits.
