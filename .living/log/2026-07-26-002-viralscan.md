### 05:40 (2026-07-27) — Mechanism found: legacy multimap never deduplicates UMIs

User asked whether Emma's version deduplicates by UMI. Checked the 2.2.0 source
(8e3e3ed) rather than inferring from the report's wording, and the answer is
partly no.

`multimap.py` parses the BUS text with a `umi` column and never uses it again:
`share = count / len(genes_in_ec)` distributes *read* counts fractionally. That
term is then summed with `counts_original`, which IS UMI-deduplicated from
`kb count`. So the archive value mixes molecules and reads. The summary labels
the multimap-only total "Total viral UMIs (corrected)" — a quantity with no UMI
collapse in it at all.

Second mixture: multimap reads raw `output.bus`, so its barcodes are not
whitelist-corrected, while the term it is added to came from the corrected path.

Explains all three anomalies: fractional values, the ~7x EBV magnitude gap, and
why skin diverges most (low-mass, multimapper-dominated, so the undeduplicated
term dominates). Recorded as F-007.

Does not establish v3 accuracy — still needs VAL-01 truth.
