Status: **not a submission draft**.

All pre-v3 quantitative results, comparative statements, private-sample analyses,
figures, and captions have been removed from this tracked file. Pre-v3 corrected
or combined counts are scientifically incompatible with the ViralScan 3.0
molecule-counting contract and cannot be relabelled or numerically migrated.

The manuscript will be regenerated only after the v3 scientific result bundle is
frozen and passes the gates in [`../PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md). Until then, no number in
an older draft is eligible for a README, abstract, result, figure, benchmark,
release, or publication claim.

## Intended contribution

The regenerated manuscript will evaluate:

- molecule-aware handling of host-virus and virus-virus ambiguity;
- combined-reference versus irreversible two-step workflows;
- exact read lineage and competitive host-plus-target evidence QC;
- reproducible reference provenance and host-genome homology safeguards; and
- candidate, probable, and strong evidence tiers calibrated on separate training
  data and evaluated once on an untouched holdout.

These are intended analyses, not completed scientific findings.

## Required artifact inputs

The manuscript generator must consume only the frozen v3 result bundle and its
manifested inputs. Every numeric or comparative statement must resolve through the
claim registry to an artifact digest, generation command, software and schema
version, Git SHA, input and reference fingerprints, count layer, denominator, and
uncertainty calculation.

At minimum, the frozen bundle must include the truth-panel holdout, harmonized
ViralScan and comparator runs, failure records, sample-level uncertainty,
reference audits, resource measurements, and the reassessed host-response example.

## Prohibited carry-over

Do not restore any of the following from a pre-v3 draft:

- corrected-count totals or fold-change claims;
- infection-rate or host-response performance values;
- private or unpublished human-subject sample identifiers or results;
- dedicated-tool, aligner, sensitivity, specificity, or superiority claims; or
- anellovirus biological-signal claims without orthogonal validation.

Private clinical data remain excluded unless the authors provide a journal-compliant
accession or controlled-access statement, ethics approval and protocol, consent
basis, and approved data-availability language.

## Author-supplied requirements

Before submission, the authors must approve author order, affiliations, ORCIDs,
CRediT roles, lead contact, funding, conflicts, acknowledgments, ethics and consent,
data availability, software and benchmark archive DOIs, and licence statements.

The final manuscript must then pass independent methods/statistics review and a
claim-to-artifact audit with no unresolved finding.
